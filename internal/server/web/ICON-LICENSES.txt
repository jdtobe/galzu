Galzu mark (Trilobe)
--------------------
Original SVG artwork created for this project: three leaves on a stem, from
docs/design/art-directions/mark/trilobe-small.svg. It replaces the previous
third-party Avataaars avatar and the provisional profile silhouette; no
third-party paths or artwork are included.
Three leaves on a stem, one colour, no background tile. The sidebar uses the
SVG as a CSS mask so its colour follows the theme.
